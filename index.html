1. Technical Foundations

To master Kelly, you must speak the language of probability with precision.

Odds Against
q / p
Ratio of losing outcomes to winning outcomes.
Dice Example: Odds against rolling a "6" are 5 to 1. You have 5 ways to lose and 1 way to win.
Decimal Odds
Total Return / Stake
Total cash returned including original stake.
Dice Example: A "fair" payout for a single number is 6.00. You get $6 total for every $1 you bet.
B Factor (Payout)
Decimal - 1
Net profit per unit of stake.
Dice Example: If you bet on "Even" (2.00 odds), your b factor is 1. You win $1 profit for every $1 bet.
Edge
bp - q
Expected net profit per unit stake.
Dice Example: Win on 1-4 (66.6% win). Payout is 2.00 (b=1). Edge = (1 * 0.66) - 0.33 = 0.33.
Kelly Criterion (f*)
Edge / b
The growth-maximizing fraction of wealth.
Dice Example: With a 33% Edge and 2.00 payout (b=1), Kelly says bet 33% of your bankroll.

2. Triple Conversion Mastery

Pros switch between these three formats in their head using the "Stake + Profit" mental model.

🧠 Intuitive Conversion: The Dice Rule

Imagine a 6-sided die. You bet $1 on the number "6".

1. Odds Against (The Ratio): There are 5 ways to lose and 1 way to win.
The Ratio: 5:1.

2. Decimal Odds (The Total): If you win, you get $5 profit plus your $1 stake back.
The Total: 6.00 ($5 + $1).

The Mental Shortcut:
• To get Decimal: Add 1 to the Ratio (5:1 → 5+1 = 6.00).
• To get Ratio: Subtract 1 from Decimal (4.00 → 4-1 = 3:1).

Probability %
How often you win.
Shortcut: 100 / Total Pieces (5:1 has 6 pieces → 16.6%)
Odds Against (X:1)
Profit relative to Stake.
Shortcut: "Losing sides to Winning sides."
Decimal Odds
Total multiplier of your money.
Shortcut: (Ratio + 1). Think "Total Payout."

2. Edge Calculation Mastery

Edge is the mathematical superiority of your win chance over the odds offered. There are three primary ways to calculate it:

Approach 1: The Multiplier (Most Robust)

Ideal for any decimal odds.

Edge = (Win% × Decimal Odds) - 1

Approach 2: Win Benefit vs Loss Penalty

Great for head math with profit ratios.

Edge = (Win% × b) - Loss%

Approach 3: Probability Gap (Even Money)

Only for 2.00 odds (Even money).

Edge = Win% - Loss%
Example: Dice Game

You win if you roll 1 or 2 (33.3% chance). Payout is 4.00 (3 to 1).

  • Method 1: (0.333 × 4.00) - 1 = 1.332 - 1 = 33.2% Edge
  • Method 2: (0.333 × 3) - 0.666 = 0.999 - 0.666 = 33.3% Edge

5. Kelly for Stock Trading

In stock trading, the goal is to determine Allocation and Leverage. Since stocks rarely go to zero, we use the average gain and average loss per trade.

S&P 500: +1.2% NASDAQ: +2.4% TSLA: -3.5%

The Finance Position Formula

Allocation = Edge / Stop Loss %

Where Edge = (Win% * WinSize%) - (Loss% * LossSize%)

Example: Tech Trade Positioning
  • Strategy: 60% win rate. Target +10% gain. Stop loss -5% loss.
  • 1. Calculate Edge: (0.6 * 10%) - (0.4 * 5%) = 6% - 2% = 4% Edge
  • 2. Position Size: 4% Edge / 5% Stop Loss = 0.80 (80% Allocation)
Leverage Guide

If the Kelly calculation exceeds 100%, use leverage.
Example: Edge of 6% with a 4% stop loss = 150% (1.5x leverage).
